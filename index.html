Executive Summary

Welcome to CelebrateHub ("we", "our", or "us"). CelebrateHub is an event services marketplace and management platform connecting event hosts ("Customers") with local celebration service providers ("Vendors" or "Providers"), including caterers, venues, photographers, decorators, DJs, and entertainment specialists.

We believe in total transparency. We only collect the minimal personal data required to match you with top-tier celebration vendors, manage your event registries and guest lists, process secure subscription billing, and authenticate your account securely.

Secure Google OAuth

🚫

Never Sold to Brokers

Precise Vendor Matching

Event locations and vendor coverage radius are used solely to match you with nearby verified service providers.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information depending on your interaction with CelebrateHub and whether you register as an Event Host (Customer) or a Service Provider (Vendor):

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and encrypted password (if registering via standard email/password authentication).
  • Customer Event Information: Event type (Wedding, Birthday, Anniversary, Corporate, Baby Shower, Festival, etc.), event title, event date and time, venue address, guest count, and estimated budget.
  • Service Requirements: Selected service categories (e.g. Photography, Catering, Decoration, Music/DJ, Makeup) and custom notes shared with matched vendors.

  • Reviews & Ratings: Post-booking vendor reviews, comments, and ratings submitted on the platform.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Telemetry Data: Mobile device model, operating system version, Expo platform identifiers, IP address, and application crash diagnostics.
  • Usage Data: In-app navigation actions, lead response times, notification status (delivered, opened), and feature engagement.

Google OAuth 2.0 User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

CelebrateHub allows users to authenticate seamlessly and securely using Google Sign-In / Google OAuth 2.0. We value your digital privacy and ensure full compliance with Google's strict developer requirements.

CelebrateHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

A. Google OAuth Scopes Requested

When you choose to sign in with Google, CelebrateHub requests only basic, non-sensitive identity scopes:

Scope Identifier Data Accessed Purpose & Usage in CelebrateHub
openid Unique Google Subject Identifier (User ID) Verifies your identity cryptographically and links your CelebrateHub account.
.../auth/userinfo.email Primary Google Email Address & Verification Status Account creation, login authentication, sending booking confirmations, lead alerts, and critical security notices.
.../auth/userinfo.profile Full Name & Profile Picture (Avatar URL) Autofills your public profile name and avatar to personalize your dashboard and vendor interactions.

B. How We Use and Protect Google User Data

  • Authentication & Account Provisioning: We use your Google User ID, Name, and Email to create and authenticate your account on CelebrateHub.
  • No Unnecessary Permissions: CelebrateHub does NOT request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Contacts, Google Photos, or Google Calendar without explicit additional user consent.
  • No Sale of Google User Data: We will never sell, lease, transfer, or distribute your Google user data to data brokers, ad networks, or unaffiliated commercial entities.
  • No Human Inspection: No CelebrateHub employee, contractor, or partner reads or inspects personal user data obtained from Google APIs, except if required by law or with your explicit request for technical support.
  • No Model Training: Data obtained via Google APIs is never used to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

How We Use Your Information & Legal Bases

We process your personal information under the following legal bases and purposes:

  • Platform Functionality & Service Delivery (Contract Performance): Matching customer events with verified providers, generating qualified leads, facilitating direct vendor bookings, and displaying real-time booking statuses.

  • Legal Compliance: Satisfying statutory tax obligations, resolving customer-vendor disputes, and complying with applicable law enforcement directives.
06

Location & Geolocation Data

Event planning is inherently location-dependent. CelebrateHub utilizes geolocation features strictly with your explicit device permissions:

  • Customer Event Locations: We allow customers to specify event addresses or drop pins on interactive maps so matched providers know where services must be rendered.
  • Provider Coverage Radius: Service providers define their operational base and coverage radius (e.g. 25km radius). We compare geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) to only dispatch leads to providers who service that area.
  • No Passive Background Tracking: CelebrateHub does not passively track your real-time GPS location in the background when the app is closed. Location data is only accessed when you interact with map features or search for local providers.
07

Sharing & Third-Party Services

We only share information under strict, limited conditions necessary to operate our marketplace:

  • Between Matched Customers and Providers: When a customer submits an event service request and a provider accepts the lead, relevant contact details (name, phone number, event details) are shared between the parties to enable direct service delivery and contract execution.
  • Database & Cloud Infrastructure: Our databases are hosted with Neon (PostgreSQL serverless cloud infrastructure) with mandatory SSL/TLS encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Payment Processors: We partner with Razorpay for subscription billing and payment processing. CelebrateHub does not store full credit card numbers or banking passwords on our servers. Razorpay processes all financial transactions in compliance with PCI-DSS Level 1 standards.
  • Google Identity Services: Used strictly for OAuth 2.0 user authentication as described in Section 4.
  • Legal & Safety Requirements: We may disclose information if required by a court subpoena, search warrant, or regulatory investigation, or to protect the vital safety of users and prevent platform fraud.
08

Security & Data Protection Safeguards

We employ industry-standard technical, operational, and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the CelebrateHub mobile app, backend servers, and external APIs are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. We utilize one-way salted bcryptjs hashing.
  • Token-Based Authentication: Sessions are managed via short-lived JSON Web Tokens (JWT) paired with rotatable refresh tokens stored in secure, encrypted device storage (Expo SecureStore on mobile devices).
  • Database Protections: PostgreSQL connection strings enforce strict SSL mode (sslmode=require), parameterized queries via Prisma ORM preventing SQL injection, and granular role-based authorization middleware.
  • Network Rate Limiting: Endpoints are protected with automated rate limiters and security headers (Helmet) to defend against brute force, DDoS, and injection vulnerabilities.

Data Retention Policy

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide CelebrateHub services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Active Accounts: User account data, business profiles, and event archives are retained while your account remains active.
  • Inactive Accounts: Accounts inactive for more than 24 consecutive months may be flagged for archival or scheduled deletion after advance email notice.
  • Financial Records: Transaction and payment logs are retained for statutory periods (typically 5 to 7 years) as required by financial, tax, and accounting legislation.
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon an approved account deletion request, personal identifiers are purged or anonymized within 30 days, subject to regulatory retention mandates.

Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Google Play Store policies, Google Cloud OAuth requirements, and international privacy standards, CelebrateHub provides two straightforward mechanisms to permanently delete your account and associated data:

1

Option A: Instant Deletion Inside the Mobile App

Open the CelebrateHub app → Navigate to Profile → Select Account Settings → Tap Delete Account & Data → Confirm your deletion. Your account, sessions, events, and personal profile information will be queued for immediate permanent removal.

2

Option B: Email Deletion Request (Direct Support)

Send an email from your registered email address (or your connected Google account email) to sairiteshdomakuntla@gmail.com with the subject line "CelebrateHub Account and Data Deletion Request".

Please include your registered full name and phone number. Our privacy team will verify your ownership and process the complete erasure of your records within 7 business days (and no later than 30 calendar days).

For more details, visit our dedicated User Data Deletion Request Page.

Cookies & Local Storage

Our mobile application does not use traditional tracking cookies. Instead, we utilize secure local storage primitives:

  • Expo SecureStore: Secure, hardware-backed keychain/keystore encryption for maintaining authentication refresh tokens between application restarts.
  • Client Memory (Zustand): In-memory application state management that is cleared upon app termination or logout.
  • Public Web Registries: Public event Gift Circle pages may store ephemeral session cookies strictly required for CSRF protection and fraud prevention.
