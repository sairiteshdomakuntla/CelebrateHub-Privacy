1. Introduction & Scope

CelebrateHub ("we", "our", or "us") provides an event services marketplace and management platform connecting event hosts ("Customers") with local celebration service providers ("Vendors" or "Providers"), including caterers, venues, photographers, decorators, and entertainment professionals.

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of personal data across the CelebrateHub mobile applications (iOS and Android), server infrastructure, and public event registry pages. By creating an account or accessing the platform, you acknowledge the terms described herein.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, as well as data generated automatically through platform usage.

2.1 User-Provided Data

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, email address, phone number, and encrypted credentials.
  • Customer Event Information: Event type (e.g. Wedding, Birthday, Corporate), event date and time, venue address, guest count, and budget range.
  • Vendor Profiles: Business legal name, business description, service categories, pricing ranges, service radius (km), geographic location coordinates, portfolio photographs, and availability schedules.
  • Gift Circle & Digital Registries: Wishlist items, external merchant links, target contribution values, and guest blessing messages.
  • Platform Reviews: Post-booking vendor reviews, comments, and ratings submitted by verified customers.

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • Device & Telemetry Data: Mobile device identifiers, operating system version, Expo platform markers, IP address, and application crash diagnostics.
  • Usage Information: In-app navigation logs, lead response timestamps, and notification delivery states.

3. Google OAuth 2.0 & User Data Policy

CelebrateHub integrates Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0) to provide secure, frictionless account registration and authentication. We strictly limit our request to standard, non-sensitive identity scopes.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance Statement
CelebrateHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3.1 Scopes Requested

The following OAuth 2.0 scopes are requested during Google Sign-In:

OAuth Scope Data Obtained Purpose in CelebrateHub
openid Unique Google Subject Identifier Cryptographically authenticates the user and verifies account identity.
.../auth/userinfo.email Primary Email & Verification State Account creation, passwordless login, and essential booking/lead notifications.
.../auth/userinfo.profile Display Name & Profile Photo URL Autofills the public user profile to display during vendor and host interactions.

3.2 Restrictions on Google User Data

  • No Scope Expansion: CelebrateHub does not request or access Gmail messages, Google Drive documents, Google Contacts, or Google Calendar records.
  • No Sale or Commercial Transfer: Google user data is never sold, licensed, or transferred to third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or marketing aggregators.
  • No Generalized AI Model Training: Personal data received from Google APIs is never utilized to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Human Review Prohibited: No internal staff or contractors inspect user data obtained through Google APIs, except upon the user's explicit request for technical support or as required by applicable law.

4. How We Use Information & Legal Bases

We process personal data under the following legitimate business purposes and legal bases:

  • Contract Performance: Connecting event hosts with eligible local vendors, distributing service leads, facilitating bookings, and administering digital gift registries.
  • Authentication & Account Integrity: Validating session requests, issuing signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT), rotating refresh tokens, and preventing fraudulent accounts.
  • Service Communications: Transmitting transactional updates regarding lead claims, booking confirmations, subscription renewals, and administrative notices.
  • Payment Processing: Managing vendor subscription tiers and customer premium event capabilities through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.
  • Statutory Compliance: Maintaining accounting ledgers, resolving consumer disputes, and fulfilling mandatory legal reporting requirements.

5. Location & Geolocation

Event service fulfillment requires geographic context. Location data is processed strictly as follows:

  • Event Locations: Customers provide an event address or interactive map pin-drop to establish the event venue.
  • Provider Coverage Radius: Vendors define their operational base and service radius (e.g. 25km). We match coordinates (latitude/longitude) to dispatch leads only to providers servicing that vicinity.
  • No Background Tracking: CelebrateHub does not track device GPS coordinates in the background when the application is closed. Location services are invoked strictly during active user sessions with explicit device authorization.

6. Third-Party Sharing

CelebrateHub does not sell or lease personal information. Data is shared exclusively with verified parties under contractual safeguards:

  • Matched Customers & Vendors: Once a lead is accepted or a booking is initiated, relevant contact details (name, phone number, event venue) are exchanged between the parties to enable direct service delivery.
  • Database Hosting (Neon): Encrypted PostgreSQL serverless infrastructure with strict SSL requirements (sslmode=require).
  • Payment Gateway (Razorpay): PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processor handling subscriptions and platform billing. CelebrateHub does not store full credit card numbers or banking secrets.
  • Google Identity Services: Invoked strictly for user authentication as set forth in Section 3.
  • Law Enforcement & Legal Process: Disclosures made solely where compelled by court order, subpoena, or applicable regulatory mandate.

7. Security & Storage Architecture

We maintain technical and organizational safeguards to ensure data confidentiality and integrity:

  • Transit Encryption: All communications between the mobile application, web clients, and backend APIs are secured with Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are one-way hashed using salted bcryptjs. Plaintext passwords are never logged or stored.
  • Secure Device Storage: Mobile refresh tokens are isolated in hardware-backed secure storage via Expo SecureStore.
  • Database Protections: Parameterized queries via Prisma ORM preventing SQL injection vulnerabilities, enforced role-based access control, and endpoint rate limiting.

8. Data Retention Policy

Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Active Accounts: Maintained for the duration of active account registration.
  • Inactive Records: Accounts without activity for more than 24 consecutive months are scheduled for review and potential archival.
  • Financial Records: Transaction ledgers and tax records are retained for statutory retention periods (typically 5 to 7 years) as required by financial regulations.
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon confirmed deletion, profile records are permanently purged or anonymized within 30 days.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Users may exercise comprehensive privacy rights in accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and applicable data protection regulations:

  • Access & Portability: Request an overview or copy of your personal data stored by CelebrateHub.
  • Correction & Rectification: Update contact details, phone numbers, and vendor profiles directly via the Profile screen.
  • Erasure: Request permanent deletion of your account and personal records.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Revoke Google OAuth permissions at any time through Google Account Permissions.

10. Data Deletion Instructions

CelebrateHub provides two standardized methods to permanently erase your account and associated records:

Method A: In-App Self-Service Deletion

Open the CelebrateHub mobile app → Navigate to Profile → Account Settings → Select Delete Account & Personal Data → Confirm prompt. Your session tokens will be invalidated immediately, and records queued for complete erasure.

Method B: Direct Support Email Request

Send an email from your registered account address to celebratehub.business@gmail.com with the subject line "CelebrateHub Account and Data Deletion Request". Requests are verified and processed within 7 business days.

For detailed timelines and category breakdowns, refer to our dedicated User Data Deletion Instructions Page.

11. Storage & Cookies

CelebrateHub mobile applications do not utilize tracking cookies. Mobile session persistence is managed via secure local storage (Expo SecureStore) and in-memory client state. Public web registry pages may utilize ephemeral cookies strictly necessary for CSRF defense and session security.

12. Children's Privacy

CelebrateHub is designed for adults planning celebrations and commercial service providers. The service is not directed to individuals under 13 years of age (or under 18 for contractual vendor engagements). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. Inquiries regarding unauthorized minor accounts should be directed to celebratehub.business@gmail.com.
