01

Our Commitment to Data Erasure

CelebrateHub respects your fundamental right to be forgotten. In compliance with Google Cloud OAuth verification requirements, Google Play developer guidelines, and global privacy frameworks (such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)), you may delete your account and all associated personal data at any time without fees or penalties.

02

Two Ways to Request Account & Data Deletion

A

Method 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion (Fastest)

If you have the CelebrateHub mobile app installed on your iOS or Android device:

  1. Open the CelebrateHub application.
  2. Tap your Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap on Account Settings or Security.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account & Personal Data.
  5. Confirm the security prompt to finalize the deletion.

Your active sessions are invalidated immediately, and your profile is scheduled for permanent purge.

B

Method 2: Manual Support Email Request

If you no longer have access to the mobile application or signed in through Google OAuth on a browser:

  1. Compose an email to our data protection team: sairiteshdomakuntla@gmail.com.
  2. Set the subject to: "CelebrateHub Data Deletion Request".
  3. Send the request from the same email address associated with your CelebrateHub or Google account.
  4. Specify whether you want partial data deletion (e.g., past event records) or complete account erasure.
03

04

How to Revoke CelebrateHub Google Account Permissions

If you used Google OAuth to sign in to CelebrateHub, you can also revoke CelebrateHub's access directly through Google's centralized dashboard at any moment:

  1. Go to your Google Account management portal at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Under "Third-party apps with account access", locate CelebrateHub.
  3. Click on CelebrateHub and choose "Remove Access".

Once removed, CelebrateHub will no longer be able to refresh or receive identity information from Google on your behalf.
