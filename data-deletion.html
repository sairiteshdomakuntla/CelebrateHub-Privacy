User Data Deletion Instructions

Step-by-step instructions on how CelebrateHub account holders can request permanent deletion of their account records, Google OAuth links, and personal data.

Standard: GDPR, CCPA & Google Cloud Policy
Processing Window: 7 to 30 Calendar Days
Controller: CelebrateHub Platform
Sections
  • 1. Data Erasure Commitment
  • 2. Deletion Methods
  • 3. Data Categories Affected
  • 4. Google Account Permissions
  • 5. Direct Email Filing

1. Data Erasure Commitment

In accordance with Google OAuth verification guidelines, app store requirements, and applicable privacy laws (such as GDPR Art. 17 and CCPA § 1798.105), CelebrateHub provides straightforward, verified mechanisms for users to permanently erase their account and personal information at any time without charge.

2. Deletion Methods

Option 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion (Instant)

If you have access to the CelebrateHub mobile app:

  1. Open the CelebrateHub application.
  2. Navigate to your Profile tab.
  3. Select Account Settings.
  4. Scroll down and select Delete Account & Personal Data.
  5. Confirm your deletion prompt. Your session will terminate immediately and records will be scheduled for permanent erasure.
Option 2: Support Email Request

If you cannot access the app or registered via Google OAuth:

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to celebratehub.business@gmail.com.
  2. Set the subject to: "CelebrateHub Account and Data Deletion Request".
  3. Provide your registered full name and phone number (if applicable).
  4. Our administration team will acknowledge the request within 48 hours and finalize the purge within 7 business days.

3. Data Categories Affected

Category Treatment Timeline
Profile & Credentials (Name, Email, Phone, Password hash) Permanently purged from PostgreSQL database Immediate upon confirmation
Google OAuth Tokens & Subject Identifiers Severed and permanently deleted Immediate
Active Sessions & Device Tokens Session table cleared; refresh tokens invalidated Immediate
Customer Event Plans & Wishlists Deleted or anonymized Within 7 days
Vendor Business Information & Leads Profile unlisted; historical lead logs anonymized Within 14 days
Statutory Financial Records Encrypted transaction records retained strictly to comply with tax/statutory obligations Statutory period (5–7 years)

4. How to Revoke Google Account Access

You can also unlink CelebrateHub directly through Google's centralized security portal at any time:

  1. Navigate to myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Locate CelebrateHub under "Third-party apps with account access".
  3. Select CelebrateHub and click "Remove Access".
