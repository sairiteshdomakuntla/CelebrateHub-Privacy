03

User Roles & Responsibilities

CelebrateHub operates as a multi-sided platform accommodating two distinct user roles:

  • Event Hosts ("Customers"): Individuals organizing personal or corporate celebrations (weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events) seeking verified local vendors.
  • Service Providers ("Vendors"): Independent businesses and professionals (caterers, photographers, venues, decorators, makeup artists, DJs) offering celebration services. Vendors are independent third parties and are not employees, agents, or contractors of CelebrateHub.
04

Marketplace Mechanics & Lead Matching

CelebrateHub matches event service requests with eligible providers based on category, location, and provider availability:

  • Lead Distribution: Leads are distributed to qualified vendors on a first-come, first-served basis according to administrator-configured policy parameters.
  • Independent Contracting: CelebrateHub serves as an intermediary platform for discovery and communication. Contracts for services (e.g. food preparation, photography shoots, venue rental) are entered directly between Customers and Vendors.
  • Provider Verification: While CelebrateHub conducts administrative reviews and verification checks for vendor profiles, CelebrateHub does not guarantee the quality, punctuality, or safety of services performed by independent vendors.
